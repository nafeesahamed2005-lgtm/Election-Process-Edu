How Do Elections Work?

Welcome to DemocracyLab, an interactive prototype designed to educate you on the core phases of a democratic election. Follow the steps below to experience the process.

1. Registration

Before an election, eligible citizens must register to vote. This ensures that only authorized individuals can cast a ballot and prevents fraud.

2. Campaigning

Candidates share their platforms and try to win the support of the voters. This involves debates, advertising, and rallies.

3. Voting

On election day, registered voters cast their ballots. In this simulation, you can cast your vote and simulate others voting.

Cast Your Ballot

4. Results

After polls close, votes are tallied. The candidate with the most votes wins the election.
